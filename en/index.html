How to play

  1. Microphone locations differ between devices. Hold your device so the horagai mouthpiece points toward a microphone that responds.
  2. Keep your mouth a little way from the microphone.
  3. Blow with a steady, moderate breath. You do not need to blow excessively hard.
  4. Tilt your device slowly or shake it to explore changes in pitch.

Do not blow excessively hard into your device. Keep a little distance so moisture from your breath does not enter the microphone or charging port. Please be considerate of the people around you when playing loudly.

Frequently asked questions

I blow, but no sound plays

Make sure Horagai has permission to use the microphone in your device settings. After launch, keep the surroundings quiet for about 1.5 seconds while the app checks the ambient sound. Check where the microphone is on your device, then try a steady, moderate breath from a short distance instead of blowing hard. You can also open Settings from the horagai icon in the upper-right corner and select “Check sound” to test the speaker.

Can I adjust the app to my breath?

Open Settings from the horagai icon in the upper-right corner and choose “Match to my breath.” Follow the three short on-screen breaths. The horagai stays silent during this measurement.

How do I change the pitch?

Tilt your device forward or backward while playing. A quick shake also adds a brief pitch change. Device angle does not affect volume.

Why do I hear a muted tap when I move the device?

While you are not playing, an impact above the detection threshold can produce a muted, hand-drum-like sound, as though the shell were tapped. Avoid dropping or striking your device; enjoy this effect only with a gentle movement.

Ads reappeared after I removed them

Open Settings from the horagai icon and choose “Restore previous purchase.” You must use the same store account that made the original purchase.

Can I record or save to the cloud?

Version 1 does not provide recording, user-account, cloud-storage, or synchronization features.

Privacy

Horagai does not save or transmit raw audio or motion input. Microphone input is converted to breath strength on your device, and tilt and motion are used only as playing controls.
