Horagai Terms of Use

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Thank you for trying Horagai. These Terms of Use describe the basic rules for using the app. By downloading or using Horagai, you agree to use it in a considerate and safe way. If you do not agree, please stop using the app.

1. Entertainment use

Horagai is a small entertainment app that turns breath, device movement, and sound into a playful experience. It is not a musical-instrument course, a professional tuning tool, or a replacement for a physical horagai. The app may not respond to every breath, and it does not promise a particular note, volume, or sound on every device.

2. Safety and considerate use

Keep a little distance from the microphone and do not blow excessively hard into your phone or tablet. Keep breath moisture away from the microphone and charging port. Move the device gently, do not drop or strike it, and stop if you feel discomfort. When playing loudly, please consider the people, place, and time around you.

You are responsible for using Horagai in a way that is appropriate for your device and surroundings. Follow any safety guidance from your device manufacturer and local rules.

3. Devices, permissions, and availability

Horagai is currently provided for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 13 or later. The app needs microphone access for breath-based playing. Device microphones, speakers, operating-system versions, ambient sound, and other conditions can affect how it responds. We cannot promise that the app will work in exactly the same way on every device or in every environment.
