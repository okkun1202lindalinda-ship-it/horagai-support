Horagai Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026

1. Microphone use

Horagai uses the microphone to detect the strength of the user's breath. Raw audio from the microphone is converted to an input level on the device. The app does not record, save, or transmit that raw audio.

2. Device tilt and motion

Device tilt and motion are used to control pitch and detect a movement like tapping the shell. The app does not record, save, or transmit this information.

3. Advertising

When ads are enabled, Horagai uses Google Mobile Ads. For ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention, the advertising provider may process information such as device identifiers, approximate location, advertising data, product interactions, and diagnostic information. In applicable regions, the app displays an advertising-consent message and provides access to advertising privacy choices from Settings.

For details, read the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Remove Ads purchase

The store where you downloaded Horagai processes the Remove Ads purchase and purchase restoration. Horagai does not receive payment details such as credit-card numbers. The app uses the purchase status only to stop displaying ads.

5. Accounts, recording, and cloud features

Version 1 does not provide user accounts, recording, cloud storage, or synchronization.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, use the support contact page. This is a public page, so do not include personal or purchase information.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when the app or applicable requirements change. Important changes will be announced on this page or in the app.
